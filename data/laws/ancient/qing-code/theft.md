citation = "Qing Code, the statute on theft"
+++
Theft is punished by the value of what was taken: 60 blows of the bamboo for a tael or less, rising step by step to 100 blows and perpetual banishment of 3,000 li at 100 taels, and strangling at 120 taels or more. A first-time thief is also tattooed on the arm.
